# What is FixFlow?

FixFlow is one place to run a local service business — bookings, customers,
marketing, loyalty rewards and day-to-day operations — with AI doing the parts
you never get around to.

Most owners are running three or four tools that don't talk to each other: a
booking page, a spreadsheet of customers, a phone for messages, and memory for
everything else. FixFlow replaces that.

## What you actually get

- **Bookings** customers make themselves, with your real opening hours
- **A customer list** that fills itself from those bookings
- **Rewards** that bring people back, without a paper punch card
- **Marketing** that writes and sends itself when you approve it
- **An AI assistant** that answers customers and drafts the work you'd
  otherwise do at 11pm
