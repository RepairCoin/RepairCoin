# What does the AI actually do?

Concrete things, not a chatbot bolted to a dashboard.

- **Answers customers** — questions about your services, prices and
  availability, in your tone, at 11pm when you're asleep
- **Books appointments** in conversation, and reschedules them
- **Writes your marketing** — the email or the campaign, the offer, and the
  image, ready for you to approve or change
- **Tells you what changed** — takings down, a quiet week, a customer who used
  to come monthly and hasn't
- **Runs the follow-ups** you never get to: the thank-you, the review request,
  the win-back

## You stay in control

Nothing goes to a customer that you have not allowed. Campaigns are drafted for
approval, not sent. Automations are saved as drafts and only run once you
publish them. You can read everything the AI said, whenever you want.

The AI features start on Starter AI. Marketing, insights and the voice
assistant come with Growth AI. Fully automatic replies and workflows are on
Business AI.
