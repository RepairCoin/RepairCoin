# How do payments work?

Customers pay by card when they book, or in person when they arrive — your
choice, per service. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and the money goes to
your own Stripe account.

## What you can set up

- **Pay when booking** — the slot is held by someone who has committed, which is
  the simplest way to cut no-shows
- **Pay in person** — book online, settle when they arrive
- **A deposit** rather than the full amount, for bigger jobs
- **Rewards against the bill**, if a customer has points to spend

## Getting the money

Card payments run through Stripe and land in your own Stripe account on
Stripe's normal payout schedule. You connect that account once during setup.
There is no card reader to buy and nothing to install — the customer pays on
their own phone.
