# I already use something else — is switching worth it?

Only if the thing you're using stops at bookings. That's the honest answer.

Most booking tools take the appointment and leave you the rest: the follow-up,
the loyalty, the marketing, the chasing. FixFlow is the whole loop, which is
also why it's worth less to you if bookings are genuinely all you need.

## The comparison that matters

- **A booking tool** fills your calendar.
- **FixFlow** fills your calendar, then works on the customer coming back —
  rewards, follow-ups, marketing and AI replies from the same customer list.

If you're on paper or a spreadsheet, the gain is bigger and more obvious: you
stop being the booking system, and the customer list stops being something you
maintain by hand.

## Switching without the hassle

- Run both for two weeks. Take new bookings in FixFlow, let the old ones play
  out where they are.
- Import your customer list so you're not starting cold.
- Move your booking link last, once you trust it.

The trial is 14 days, which is roughly how long that takes.
