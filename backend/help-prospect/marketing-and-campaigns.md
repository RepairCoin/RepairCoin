# Can it do my marketing?

Yes — write it, target it, and send it. You approve; it does the work.

The common case: it's quiet next week, you want to fill it, and you don't want
to spend an evening writing an email nobody opens.

## People ask this as

- Can it do marketing for me?
- Can I send emails to my customers?
- How do I get more bookings?
- Can it write promotions?
- How do I bring back customers who stopped coming?
- Can it make the images too?
- Do I have to approve what it sends?

## How it works

1. **Say what you want** in a line — "fill Tuesday", "win back people who
   haven't been in a month"
2. **It writes it** — subject, body and a banner image
3. **You read it, change it, or reject it.** Nothing sends unapproved
4. **It picks who to send to** — lapsed customers, regulars, everyone
5. **You see what came back** — opens, and the bookings that followed

You can also set it to run on its own: when a customer hasn't been in for 30
days, send the win-back. Set once, runs forever, and you can switch it off.

Marketing campaigns come with Growth AI. The automated version — campaigns that
fire on their own — is on Business AI.
