# How do bookings work?

You publish your services and your opening hours. Customers pick a time that is
actually free and book it themselves. You stop being the booking system.

## People ask this as

- How do bookings work?
- Can customers book online?
- Do you have a booking system?
- Can I take appointments?
- How do I stop double bookings?
- Can customers pay when they book?
- Can I set my own hours?
- What about no-shows?

## What it handles

- **Your real availability** — opening hours per day, breaks, how long each job
  takes, and how many you can run at once
- **Holidays and one-off changes** without unpublishing anything
- **Payment at booking**, if you want it, so a slot is held by someone who has
  committed
- **No-shows**, recorded against the customer rather than forgotten
- **Rescheduling** by the customer, inside rules you set

Because bookings come in through FixFlow, your customer list builds itself. You
never type a name into a spreadsheet again.
