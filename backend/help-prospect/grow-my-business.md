# How do I grow my business faster?

Most local businesses don't grow by finding more new customers. They grow by
losing fewer of the ones they already have, and by filling the gaps in the week
they currently write off.

FixFlow is built around those two things.

## People ask this as

- How can I grow my business faster?
- How do I get more customers?
- How do I get more bookings?
- How do I make more money?
- How do I fill quiet days?
- How do I get repeat customers?
- What's the fastest way to grow?
- Can you help me grow?
- How do I stop losing customers?

## Where the growth actually comes from

- **Stop the leak.** Most customers don't leave angry, they just drift. FixFlow
  spots someone who used to come monthly and hasn't, and reaches out before
  they've found someone else.
- **Fill the quiet days.** When next week looks thin, it writes the offer and
  sends it to the people most likely to take it.
- **Make coming back worth it.** Rewards give a regular a reason to choose you
  over the place that's five minutes closer.
- **Stop losing bookings after hours.** Most enquiries arrive when you're busy
  or asleep. The AI answers and books them anyway.
- **Ask for the reviews you're owed.** Happy customers rarely think to leave
  one unless asked, at the right moment.

None of that is work you have to remember to do. You set it once.
