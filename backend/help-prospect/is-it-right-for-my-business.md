yes.

## The honest test

FixFlow fits you well if:

- People **book** you rather than walk in at random
- You want them to **come back**, and you have no reliable way to make that happen
- You are the person doing the admin, and it is eating your evenings

It fits you **badly** if:

- You sell products off a shelf with no appointments — you want a retail POS
- You take one enormous project a year — a booking system solves nothing
- You already have a team on a system everyone likes; switching costs more than it saves

We would rather say that now than sell you a trial that was never going to work.
