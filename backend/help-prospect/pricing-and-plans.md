# How much does FixFlow cost?

Four plans. All prices are per month, and every paid plan starts with a
**14-day free trial** — no charge until the trial ends.

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Trying it out — bookings and customers, no AI |
| **Starter AI** | $80 | A single owner-operator who wants rewards and an AI assistant |
| **Growth AI** | $299 | A busy shop that wants marketing, inventory and insights |
| **Business AI** | $599 | Multiple staff or locations, and automation that runs itself |

## What changes between plans

- **Free** gives you bookings and your customer list. No AI, no rewards.
- **Starter AI** adds customer rewards and the AI assistant.
- **Growth AI** adds marketing campaigns, inventory, business insights, and the
  voice assistant.
- **Business AI** adds team accounts, multiple locations, automated workflows,
  and AI that replies to customers on its own.

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of AI usage, and the allowance grows with
the plan. Ordinary use sits inside it comfortably.
