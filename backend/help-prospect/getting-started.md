# How do I get started?

Sign up, add your services and your hours, and share your booking link. Most
shops are taking bookings the same day.

Every paid plan begins with a **14-day free trial**.

## People ask this as

- How do I start?
- How do I sign up?
- How long does setup take?
- Is it hard to set up?
- Do I need to install anything?
- What happens after the trial?
- Can I import my customers?
- Can I cancel?

## The first hour

1. **Create your shop** — name, what you do, where you are
2. **Add your services** — name, price, how long each takes
3. **Set your hours**, including breaks and the days you're closed
4. **Share your booking link** — in your bio, on your Google listing, in replies

Everything else — rewards, marketing, AI, automations — can wait until bookings
are landing. Turning it all on at once is the most common way to make a first
week harder than it needs to be.

## Bringing your existing customers

You can import a customer list, so people you already know don't start from
zero. If your list lives in a phone or a notebook, start without it — the list
rebuilds itself from bookings within a few weeks.

## Nothing to install

FixFlow runs in a browser, on a phone or a laptop. Your customers don't install
anything either — they just open a link.
