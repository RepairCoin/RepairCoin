# How do customer rewards work?

Customers earn points for spending with you, and spend them on their next
visit. It is a loyalty card that cannot be lost in a wallet, and you never
stamp anything.

The points are called RCN. For you and your customers they behave like store
credit: earned at your shop, spent at your shop.

## What it means in practice

- **They earn automatically** when a job is completed. Nothing to remember.
- **They come back to spend it.** That is the whole point — a balance sitting
  with you is a reason to return rather than go elsewhere.
- **You control the cost.** You decide what to give and when; rewards are not
  issued without your say-so.
- **Regulars are worth more than the discount.** The maths only works because a
  returning customer costs nothing to win a second time.

Rewards are available on Starter AI and above. The Free plan does not include
them.

## About the crypto part

RCN is built on blockchain technology, but neither you nor your customers need
to know or care. There is no wallet to manage, nothing to trade, and no price
to watch. It works like points because that is what it is for.
